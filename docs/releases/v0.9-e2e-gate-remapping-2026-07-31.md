## 1. Decision

v0.9 release gates are remapped so **test purpose and test means stay aligned**:

- functional coverage that can be made deterministic belongs in **Playwright**;
- human feel on a few critical paths belongs in **human/Agent browser operation**;
- real-device, provider, and customer cohort evidence remain separate later gates.

This remapping does not reopen G0/G1 engineering findings. It changes what
“G2 PASS” and later gates mean for binding release evidence.

| Gate | Purpose | Means | Cadence | Blocks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## 3. G2 vs G3 boundary

### 3.1 G2 — Playwright functional net

Ask: **is the product functionally correct in the browser?**

Include when the answer is objectively yes/no:

- named business actions succeed or are denied for the right actor;
- status, amounts, relations, versions, and tenant scope are correct;
- list / detail / planning / My Work / mobile viewport stay coherent;
- refresh, retry, and duplicate submit do not corrupt state;
- desktop Chromium and mobile viewport profiles for the same journey.

Source material: manual suite cases 01–04 and 07 stages that assert correctness.
Those cases remain the product checklist; Playwright becomes the binding runner
wherever selectors and environments are stable.

### 3.2 G3 — Human/Agent experience sample

Ask: **can a person find the next step and trust the surface?**

Keep this gate deliberately narrow:

- a small set of critical paths (not full suite coverage);
- multiple roles, enough to feel cross-role continuity;
- affordance, copy, loading/empty/error/success clarity, visual hierarchy;
- findings that are functional P0/P1 must be fixed and, when automatable,
  added back into **G2** so they do not depend on re-clicking forever.

G3 is not a substitute for G2 breadth. Agent browser operation is scaffolding
for discovery and G3 evidence, not the long-term merge gate.

## 5. CRM foundation conventions (v0.9)

These product conventions are binding for G2-S0 and later commercial journeys.

1. **Lead is a stage, not an object.** Contact and Company use `lifecycle_stage`;
   there is no separate `lead` business object.
2. **Reactive Repair / Callout creates Customers.** New Company and Contact
   records used on the v0.9 repair/callout path must be saved with
   `lifecycle_stage = customer`. Marketing-funnel stages remain available but
   are **not** required G2 coverage for this path.
3. **An executable Work Order’s party is a Customer.** Seeded demo parties may
   still be `lead` for historical fixtures; fresh G2 journeys must not leave
   an in-progress Work Order hanging off a lead-stage party.
4. **Two-step create is accepted for v0.9 G2.** Operators create Company /
   Contact / Service Site on their own forms, then attach them via Quote or
   Work Order lookups. Inline “create while composing Quote/WO” is product
   debt for a later iteration and is out of G2 close.
5. **Lead→customer conversion Commands** and sales-funnel automation remain
   v1.0 qualification scope; v0.9 G2 asserts the persisted stage value, not a
   governed conversion workflow.

| G2-S5 | Role denial smoke | Mixed | unauthorized actor cannot perform approve/dispatch/pay actions from UI | Complements G1 authz; UI must not imply success |

**Out of v0.9 G2 close (explicit)**

- inline create of Company/Contact/Site inside Quote/WO composers;
- governed lead-qualification / stage-conversion Commands;
- marketing-funnel stage breadth beyond setting and asserting `customer`.

**v0.9 G2 exit rule**

- G2-S0 through G2-S5 PASS (or an explicitly scoped `N/A` with product sign-off);
- unresolved automated P0/P1 = 0;
- artifact retains exact SHA, clean tree, traces/screenshots, and fresh record IDs;
- projection-only evidence alone cannot close G2.

## 7. G3 minimal sample (not full coverage)

For the first G3 binding run, limit to four critical paths:

1. Owner canonical happy path (feel of end-to-end next actions);
2. Quote multi-role approval loop (findability of approve/return/send);
3. Technician mobile field completion (mobile `/m` clarity);
4. Customer pay / access moment (trust and copy).

Each path records affordance/experience findings. Functional defects gate the
release only after severity triage; automatable defects become G2 cases.

## 8. G4 / G5 unchanged intent

- **G4** still requires retained device/provider evidence that Playwright
  desktop automation cannot honestly claim (installed PWA, real push opt-in,
  provider reconciliation invariants).
- **G5** still requires 3–5 customer 90/10 and upgrade/repeatability records
  with zero customer-specific Core forks.
