## Stance

G3 asks whether a person with a job to do can **find the next step and trust
the surface**. This catalog treats the Agent browser sample as an **experience
audit**, not a functional re-test of G2.

Role lenses used:

- **Owner / office** — Quote → WO → Invoice → ask customer to pay
- **Sales Manager** — recognize and approve the right Quote
- **Technician** — pick today’s visit and finish the form on `/m`
- **Customer** — understand job status and pay without Runory jargon

Severity follows [manual E2E](../testing/manual-e2e/README.md) §4. Items marked
**UX-P1** are experience majors (wrong actor, mischarge risk, unrecognizable
work) even when the governed path can still complete.

## A. Detail pages (Quote / Work Order / Invoice)

| ID | Sev | Problem | Why it hurts | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UX-A1 | UX-P1 | Page H1 is the **object type** (“Work Order”, “Quotes”), not quote number / job title | Operator loses orientation; must hunt Basic Info for identity | `ObjectDetailPage` uses `record.name ?? title` where `title` is the type label |

| UX-A6 | P2 | On Work Order, default payment amount can be **0** with no WO total helper | Operator invents cents without commercial context | payment prefill logic |

## Recommended fix order (experience, not G2 automation)

1. **Trust & money (UX-P1):** A2 minor→major currency UI; A3 request payment = copyable customer link (don’t hijack operator); D1 provider-first portal brand; D2 hide persona on `/access`.
2. **Recognize the work (UX-P1):** A1 record identity in H1; B1 / C1 / C2 subject titles + human assignee/site on cards.
3. **Lifecycle clarity (P2):** A4 hide or collapse payment until eligible; D3/D4 schedule truth from visits; B2 Claim vs Approve.
4. **Field continuity (P2):** C7 form → visit; C5/C6 copy and CTA density; A5/E4 i18n.

Automatable regressions (e.g. persona absent on `/access`, no raw `usr_` on My Work for seeded tech) can later fold into **G2**; pure copy/hierarchy stays G3 backlog.
